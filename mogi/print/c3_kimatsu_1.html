1　現在完了の意味　記号で答える／各1点

下線部の文の意味を、ア「ずっと〜している」（継続）／イ「〜したことがある」（経験）／ウ「〜したところだ・〜してしまった」（完了）から選び、記号を書きなさい。

  1. I have lived in this town for ten years.
  2. Have you ever seen a panda?
  3. I have just finished my homework.
  4. She has been sick since last week.
  5. He has never been to Okinawa.
  6. Has the train left yet?

2　並べかえ　各3点

［　］内の語（句）を正しく並べかえて、対話文を完成させなさい。文全体を書くこと。

  1. A: How long have you lived here?　B: I have / lived / here / since 2015.
  2. A: Have / you / ever / been to Kyoto?　B: Yes, twice.
  3. A: Where is Ken?　B: He has / just / left / home.
  4. A: Do you know who / made / this / cake?　B: My sister did.
  5. A: Is Emi at home?　B: I don't know where / she / is / now.
  6. A: This picture is beautiful.　B: Yes. It was / taken / by / my father.
  7. A: Can you show / me / how / to use this camera?　B: Sure.
  8. A: Was the story difficult?　B: Yes, but Lucy helped / me / understand / it.

オモテ 2

  1. I have lived here since 2015.
  2. Have you ever been to Kyoto?
  3. He has just left home.
  4. Do you know who made this cake?
  5. I don't know where she is now.
  6. It was taken by my father.
  7. Can you show me how to use this camera?
  8. Yes, but Lucy helped me understand it.

オモテ 3

(1) since　(2) for　(3) ever　(4) never　(5) yet　(6) already

オモテ 4　主語・動詞・時制がそろっていれば○。studied／been studying どちらも可

  1. I have studied [been studying] English for three years.
  2. Have you ever climbed Mt. Fuji?
  3. I don't know where he lives.
  4. This book is read by many people.

ウラ A

  2. No, she hasn't. [No, she has never been there.]

ねらい：オモテは 現在完了（継続・経験・完了）・間接疑問・受け身・help ＋人＋原形・how to。ウラは 2学期末の模試 341〜352 で「解答欄に自分で書く」語（曜日・数・年号・校内の場所・過去分詞・Would you like／Shall we／How much・疑問詞・主語と動詞のある答え方）と、その仲間の語。
