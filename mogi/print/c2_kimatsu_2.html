  2. I came here to meet my cousin.
  3. I have nothing to drink now.

  5. Does he have to work on Sunday?

オモテ 4　主語・動詞がそろっていれば○

  1. What time is it in Japan?
  2. I want something to eat.
  3. I went there to borrow some books. [Because I wanted to borrow some books.]
  4. I don't know how to use this computer.

ウラ C
