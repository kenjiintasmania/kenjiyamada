解答　中2 2学期末対策 ⑤⑥

配点：オモテ50点＋ウラ50点

オモテ 1

(1) イ　(2) ア　(3) ウ　(4) イ　(5) ア　(6) ウ

オモテ 2

  1. I want to play tennis with my friends.
  2. I got up early to walk my dog.
  3. I have a lot of dishes to wash.
  4. Let's buy something hot to eat.
  5. You must be quiet in the library.
  6. Do we have to bring lunch tomorrow?
  7. I don't know what to do next.
  8. You don't have to worry about it.

オモテ 3

① want　② have　③ how　④ to　⑤ must　⑥ will

オモテ 4　主語・動詞がそろっていれば○

  1. How is the weather in London?
  2. I want something to read.
  3. I went there to play soccer. [Because I wanted to play soccer.]
  4. She has to clean her room.

ウラ A

ウラ B

eight / nine / twelve / fourteen / forty / ninety / three hundred / two thousand

ウラ C

ウラ E

  2. She will visit her aunt on November 3.
