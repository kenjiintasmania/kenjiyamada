1　現在完了の意味　記号で答える／各1点

下線部の意味を ア「ずっと〜している」／イ「〜したことがある」／ウ「〜したところだ」 から選び、記号で。

  1. We have been friends since we were children.
  2. I have read this book three times.
  3. The bus has already left.
  4. She has studied English for five years.
  5. Have you ever eaten natto?
  6. I haven't finished my lunch yet.

2　並べかえ　［　］内を並べかえて文全体を書く／各3点

  1. A: How long have you been here?　B: I have / been / here / for an hour.
  2. A: Has / she / ever / visited Nara?　B: Yes, once.
  3. A: Are you hungry?　B: No. I have / already / eaten / lunch.
  4. A: Do you know what / time / it / is?　B: It's three.
  5. A: Please tell me why / he / was / late.　B: He missed the bus.
  6. A: Who wrote this letter?　B: It was / written / by / my grandmother.
  7. A: Could you tell / me / how / to get to the museum?　B: Sure.

4　場面に合う英文を書く（期末 大問3の形）　各2点

  1. 友達に「ケーキを食べませんか」とすすめる。（Would you like で始めて）
