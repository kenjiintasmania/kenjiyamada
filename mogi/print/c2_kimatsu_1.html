1　不定詞のはたらき　記号で答える／各1点

下線部のはたらきを、ア「〜すること」（名詞）／イ「〜するために」（目的）／ウ「〜するための・〜すべき」（名詞を説明）から選び、記号を書きなさい。

  1. I want to be a doctor.
  2. She went to the library to study.
  3. I have a lot of homework to do.
  4. To swim in the sea is fun.
  5. Do you have anything to drink?
  6. He got up early to make breakfast.

2　並べかえ（Xコースの形）　各3点

［　］内の語（句）を正しく並べかえて、対話文を完成させなさい。文全体を書くこと。

  1. A: Where do you want to go?　B: I want to / visit / Kyoto / someday.
  2. A: Why did you go to the station?　B: I went there to / see / my / uncle.
  3. A: Are you busy today?　B: Yes. I have a lot of / work / to / do.
  4. A: I'm thirsty.　B: Here you are. I have something / cold / to / drink.
  5. A: Do / have / we / to wear a uniform at the festival?　B: Yes, we do.
  6. A: You must / not / here / swim. It's dangerous.　B: I see.
  7. A: What is your dream?　B: My dream is / to / be / a nurse.
  8. A: We still have time.　B: Good. We don't / have / to / hurry.

3　1語を入れる　各2点

  1. I don't know to say.　何を言えばよいか
  2. Please tell me to get to the station.　行き方
  3. It's time go to bed.
  4. You don't to come early.　来なくてよい
  5. He study hard for the test.　〜しなければならない・1語
  6. I want to drink.　何か飲むもの

4　英語で書く　各2点

  1. 私は友達に会うために公園へ行きました。
  2. 何か食べるものを持っていますか。
  3. 今日は宿題をしなければなりません。（have を使って）
  4. 彼女は英語を勉強するためにアメリカへ行くつもりです。（be going to を使って）

解答　中2 2学期末対策 ①②

配点：オモテ50点＋ウラ50点

オモテ 1

(1) ア　(2) イ　(3) ウ　(4) ア　(5) ウ　(6) イ

オモテ 2

  1. I want to visit Kyoto someday.
  2. I went there to see my uncle.
  3. I have a lot of work to do.
  4. I have something cold to drink.
  5. Do we have to wear a uniform at the festival?
  6. You must not swim here.
  7. My dream is to be a nurse.
  8. We don't have to hurry.

オモテ 3

(1) what　(2) how　(3) to　(4) have　(5) must　(6) something

オモテ 4　主語・動詞・不定詞がそろっていれば○。see／meet どちらも可

  1. I went to the park to see [meet] my friend(s).
  2. Do you have anything to eat?
  3. I have to do my homework today.
  4. She is going to go to America to study English.

ウラ A

Monday / Tuesday / Wednesday / Thursday / Friday / Saturday / Sunday / December　　November / January / weekend / afternoon

ウラ B

five / ten / twelve / fifteen / thirty / forty-five / (one [a]) hundred / half / second

ウラ C

(1) in　(2) at　(3) for　(4) going　(5) If　(6) to　(7) will　(8) on　(9) at　(10) half

ウラ D

playing / making / inviting / swimming / running / sitting / studying / writing / going

ウラ E

(1) Where　(2) How many　(3) What time　(4) What day　(5) How long　(6) When　(7) What　(8) Why

ウラ F

  1. He will stay in Japan [there] for two weeks.
  2. She will come to Japan on March 10.

ねらい：オモテは to不定詞3用法と must／have to（Xコース）。ウラは 2学期末の模試 231・232 で「解答欄に自分で書く」語（曜日・数・in／at／for・動名詞・疑問詞・主語と動詞のある答え方）と、その仲間の語。
